WE ARE

THE BEGINNING

BORN FROM QUIET STREETS

In 2021, [STOIC] was born in the alleyways of Manila — not from a boardroom or a trend forecast, but from a genuine need for clothing that speaks without shouting.

Our founder, a graphic designer tired of fast fashion's empty promises, started screen-printing tees in a small garage. The philosophy was simple: wear less, mean more.

Today, [STOIC] has grown into a full streetwear brand with a community of thousands who share the same belief — that real style is about intention, not imitation.

THE DESIGN

ART THAT LIVES ON FABRIC

Every graphic in a [STOIC] piece is drawn by hand, distilled from philosophy, street culture, and raw emotion. We work with local Filipino artists who understand that clothing is the canvas of everyday life.

We don't chase trends. We set them quietly — releasing limited drops that create real desire, not manufactured hype. When a [STOIC] piece sells out, it's gone forever.

♻

SUSTAINABLE

We use GOTS-certified organic cotton and water-based inks. Our packaging is 100% recyclable, and we're working toward carbon-neutral shipping by 2026.

🤝

ETHICAL

Every worker in our supply chain earns a living wage. We partner exclusively with factories that meet international labor standards and treat their teams with dignity.

🎨

LOCAL FIRST

From design to production, we keep it local. [STOIC] supports Filipino artists, printers, and manufacturers — keeping the money in the community that made us.
